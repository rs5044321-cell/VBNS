WHO WE ARE

A Legacy of Educational Integrity & Excellence

Established with a vision to revolutionize primary and secondary education, Vandey Bharti National School combines rigorous academic methodologies with cutting-edge student administration tools. We believe that true education goes beyond textbooks—it lies in fostering curiosity, building character, and developing modern global citizens.

Holistic Academic Pedagogy

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE

Our Curriculum & Grades

We offer comprehensive academic pathways from Primary School to Senior Secondary Board Examinations, maintaining world-class standards.

Primary (Grades I - V)

Fostering basic literacy, numeracy, creative expression, and curiosity through highly interactive play-based learning activities.

Middle School (Grades VI - VIII)

Transitioning into robust conceptual knowledge in mathematics, natural sciences, language boards, and computer literacy.

Senior Secondary (Grades IX - XII)

Advanced specializations in Science, Commerce, and Humanities. Aligned with Board Examinations and college-prep pathways.
